# Turf Design Analysis Notes

## Visual Identity

**Logo:** Chunky, rounded, bubbly white lowercase wordmark "turf" on deep green (#1B6B3E). Feels playful, approachable, and sporty without being aggressive. The letterforms have personality, almost hand-drawn quality.

**Primary Color:** Deep field green (like actual turf grass). Used as the brand color in backgrounds, accents, and marketing materials.

**UI Mode:** Primarily dark mode with rich blacks and subtle gradient overlays. The dark background makes the content cards pop.

## Card Design (The "Ticket Stub" Pattern)

This is Turf's signature UI element. Each game/event is presented as a card that looks like a physical sports ticket:
- Rounded corners with a perforated dashed line separating sections (like a tear-off stub)
- Subtle gradient backgrounds on cards (team colors bleeding into each other)
- Clear visual hierarchy: team logos on sides, event info centered, metadata below the dashed line
- The dashed line separator is a brilliant touch: it divides "the matchup" from "the details" (players, squads, prizes)

## Typography

- Bold, condensed sans-serif for numbers and scores (large, high-contrast)
- Clean system-style sans-serif for body text
- "WEEK" labels in small caps, numbers large and bold below
- Horizontal scrolling week selector with clear active state

## Information Architecture

- Scoreboard at top (always visible during live games)
- Tab navigation: Questions, Play-by-Play, Stats, Standings
- Questions presented as clean cards with clear answer options
- Point badges (+10 points in green, -2 points in red) are small, pill-shaped, color-coded
- Live chat/commentary bar at the bottom with user avatars

## Motion and Interaction Cues

- Countdown timers with monospaced numerals (7M 36s)
- "?" placeholders for unknown attendees (mystery/anticipation)
- Avatar stacks showing community participation (+400, +35 people)
- Notification bell with red dot indicator

## Light Mode Version

The light mode uses:
- White/cream background
- Soft iridescent/holographic gradients on cards (rainbow shimmer effect)
- Same ticket-stub card pattern with dashed separator
- Team color accents bleeding subtly into card backgrounds
- Clean, minimal, lots of whitespace

## Key Design Principles Observed

1. **Physical metaphors:** The ticket stub makes digital feel tangible
2. **Color as information:** Team colors communicate identity without labels
3. **Progressive disclosure:** Scoreboard > Questions > Details, layered
4. **Social proof everywhere:** Avatar stacks, participant counts, live chat
5. **Playful but precise:** Bubbly logo, but razor-sharp data presentation
6. **Reward visibility:** Points, prizes, and rankings always visible
